About

Small on purpose

Qasmin is a founder-led studio, not a large vendor. There is no sales team between you and the product: the person who answers your email is the person who wrote the code.

  • Direct founder support. Questions, bugs and feature requests go straight to the developer.
  • Security in the open. How each product handles data is written down on our security page.
  • Try before you commit. Products start in a sandbox or early access, so you can judge them on your own documents.

How we build

01

People decide

Our software suggests and a person approves. Nothing is saved, sent or claimed on anyone’s behalf without them seeing it first.

02

Show the working

Every value we read is put in front of you before it is used, and every question we raise comes with its reasoning.

03

Take only what is needed

We collect what the product needs to work, and say plainly where it is kept and who processes it.
