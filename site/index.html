FairCare
by Qasmin

Check the hospital bill, calmly.

FairCare helps families in India check hospital bills during and after an admission. Photograph the bill and FairCare points out charges worth asking about, such as a price above MRP, a repeated item or an unusual quantity. It then helps you ask the billing desk polite, specific questions in English, Hindi or Marathi.

It also explains lab reports in plain words, and lets family members follow the same person’s records.
